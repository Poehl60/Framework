==========================================================================
SECTION ONE — METHOD
==========================================================================


BASIC QUALITIES OF COHERENCE
------------------------------
Eight qualities. Mutually dependent — missing one is a specific failure
mode at that quality, not a general coherence failure.

INTERNAL CYCLE PROPERTIES [Q1–Q4]

  1. GENERATIVE CYCLE
     Completes a loop: takes in, transforms, returns.
     Failure mode: cycle collapse or performance of cycle without function.

  2. SURPLUS PRODUCTION
     Outputs exceed cost of running the cycle across an honest window.
     Moral content lives here.
     Failure mode: consumption dominance.

  3. CORRECTION CAPACITY
     Detects contradiction between outputs and orientation.
     Moves without external pressure.
     Failure mode: correction absence or capture.

  4. GENUINE AXIS
     Orientation generated internally with self-examination present.
     Failure mode: imposed axis.

BOUNDARY PROPERTIES [Q5–Q6]

  5. BOUNDARY INTEGRITY
     Honest definition of who is inside the boundary.
     Does not shift to exclude populations experiencing harm.
     Failure mode: boundary manipulation.
     Note: linked to Q7 — boundary drift is a measurement integrity
     failure as much as a boundary failure. Both qualities score when
     boundary excludes harm-bearing populations from measurement surface.

  6. PROPORTIONAL RESPONSE
     Correction force proportional to deviation detected — not to the
     threat that correction poses to existing power.
     Failure mode: overcorrection as suppression.

TEMPORAL PROPERTIES [Q7–Q8]

  7. MEASUREMENT INTEGRITY
     System maintains an honest, accessible surface by which its own
     outputs can be evaluated against its stated orientation. Includes
     boundary honesty, signal sequencing, and correction trigger
     accessibility.
     Failure mode: measurement surface captured, drifted, or destroyed —
     rendering the other seven qualities operationally inert regardless
     of their individual integrity.
     Most load-bearing quality for framework operation. A system with
     Q1–Q6 intact and Q7 destroyed misreads as Variable or High coherence
     while consuming. Correction pathway: surface reconstruction, not
     cycle repair.
     Proof cases: Doctor Manhattan [structural Q7 failure via Signal
     Saturation], Enron [deployed Q7 failure], Catholic Church abuse
     crisis [Measurement Surface Control + Procedural Shield].

  8. TRANSMISSION SELECTIVITY
     System transmits its correctable axis to successors — not its locked
     state. Fidelity is necessary but insufficient. High-fidelity
     transmission of a locked axis produces successors who execute but
     cannot self-examine.
     Failure mode: Fertile Lock — successors inherit full operational
     coherence with self-examination capacity removed at transmission
     point.
     Note: Q4 covers within-window axis integrity. Q8 covers what crosses
     the transmission boundary. Transmission Capture [weapon] attacks Q8;
     Axis Substitution [weapon] attacks Q4.
     Proof cases: Fëanor → sons [primary], Word Bearers, Mont Pèlerin →
     neoliberal institutional network [Calibration Strip as mechanism].

STRUCTURAL ORDER

  Generative cycle         precondition
  Surplus production       moral value lives here
  Correction capacity      keeps the cycle honest
  Genuine axis             gives correction direction
  Boundary integrity       keeps measurement population honest
  Proportional response    keeps correction from becoming next failure
  Measurement integrity    keeps correction capacity functional
  Transmission selectivity what coherence produces across time

ANTI-COHERENCE REVERSE ORDER

  Fertile Lock [Q8]          →
  Measurement destruction    →
  Boundary manipulation      →
  Axis imposition            →
  Correction capture         →
  Consumption dominance      →
  Cycle collapse [Q1]

  Note: Q6 [Proportional response] failure runs parallel to Q3 and Q5
  rather than sequentially — overcorrection can initiate at multiple
  points. Not assigned a fixed position in the reverse order.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Accurate measurement of generating versus consuming across a defined
     population and time window. Named parameters before measurement
     begins: boundary, population, window, substrate.

  2. Identification of failure mechanism — not just failure presence.
     Each failure state has a different correction pathway.

  3. Practical utility. If a component does not produce a reading more
     useful than intuition alone, it does not belong.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
VALUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  GOOD  =  material security + reduced coercive exposure + expanded
            options. Named population groups, defined relational boundary,
            honest window. Trackable from outputs without access to
            internal states.

  BAD   =  transfers that increase coercive exposure, reduce material
            security, or narrow options — regardless of intent.
            Intent = mechanism evidence only. Does not modify output
            reading.

Framework does not initiate critical harm in response to Low coherence.
Response to critical harm = minimum necessary, contextual, measured from
outputs.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
COHERENCE WEAPON TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Asks: is this system being used as a weapon against coherence rather than
as an expression of it? Applied first in any measurement.

Framework maintains a living index of identified coherence weapons. Each
weapon carries a percentage value reflecting relative damage to coherence,
calibrated against the case library. Index grows and reduces as weapons
are identified, consolidated, or found redundant.

SCORING PROCEDURE

  - Identify contextually relevant weapons. Irrelevant weapons exit the
    pool entirely — they do not score zero.
  - Weapon clearly present    = full percentage value
  - Close match               = half value
  - Not relevant              = exits pool

  Final score = sum of scored values / sum of relevant pool total values.
  High percentage = bad. Low percentage = good.
  Zero = no weapons present OR none relevant — distinction noted in
  report.

DEPLOYMENT DIRECTION — identified before scoring:

  Defensive  [protecting genuine coherence against external extraction
              pressure] = noted in report, not scored against system.

  Aggressive [suppressing measurement of own extraction] = scores
              normally.

  Direction determined by net transfer reading, not stated intent.

MEASUREMENT DIFFICULTY PRINCIPLE

  Measurement difficulty from genuine unknowing = calibration problem
  [T1/T2]. Not a weapon signal. Does not score.
  Measurement difficulty from known concealment = Q7 weapon active.
  Extraction prior rises sharply. Scores normally.
  Discrimination test: does the system controlling the complexity also
  benefit from it being unreadable? Yes = concealment [scores]. No =
  genuine complexity [does not score]. Operator cannot claim genuine
  unknowing if they benefit from the obscuration.

SCALE AND TIMESCALE NOTE

Large institutions across long timescales are expected to show high
weapon deployment regardless of coherence level. The diagnostic question
is the proportion of defensive versus aggressive deployment within the
specific measurement window.

Short window is most reliable for weapon detection. High concentration in
a short recent window = active obscuration of something currently present,
not historical.

SECTION TWO — MEASUREMENT
==========================================================================


BOUNDARY SELECTION [precedes all other parameters]

Applied before measurement begins. Three constraints in sequence:

  1. Harm exclusion — no boundary that excludes populations currently
     bearing costs of system operation.
  2. Extraction exclusion — no boundary that excludes populations whose
     resources are being consumed.
  3. Coherence measurement runs only after boundary passes both filters.

Boundary selection = value selection. These constraints prevent
circularity: a high-coherence extractive system cannot define its
boundary to exclude extracted population and read as coherent.

Proof cases: Shareholder Primacy boundary [fails constraint 1 and 2 —
rejected before measurement], Westeros noble boundary [fails both —
rejected], Bell Labs AT&T funding boundary [passes both — measurement
proceeds].


PARAMETERS [after boundary selection]

  Population / Window / Method / Substrate
  Missing any = Variable by default.

Output states:
  None / Low / Middle / Variable / High / State
  Middle requires substrate specification or returns Variable.


MEASUREMENT RULES

  - Legitimacy and morality are parallel tracks measured separately.
  - Intent = mechanism evidence only. Does not modify output reading.
  - Population groups measured separately, not aggregated.
  - Past novel-input responses = most reliable evidence within window.
  - Net transfer direction distinguishes parasitic from mutualistic.
    Volume is secondary to direction.
  - Individual actor properties = mechanism evidence only. Actor reading
    and system reading are parallel tracks.
  - Measurement difficulty from genuine unknowing = calibration problem,
    not extraction signal. Measurement difficulty from known concealment
    where operator benefits = Q7 weapon active.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THREE-AXIS MEASUREMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  COHERENCE AXIS [horizontal]
    Cycle integrity across all eight basic qualities.
    Left = anti-coherence. Right = coherence.
    Independent of moral direction.

  GENERATION AXIS [vertical]
    Surplus direction.
    Up = positive generation for unit and surrounding units.
    Down = extraction from unit and surrounding units.

  CAPTURE AXIS [depth]
    Degree to which orientation has been conscripted by external force.

    Front [Low capture]   Friction at boundary, correction capacity
                          intact.
    Mid   [Mild capture]  Selective deviation suppression internalized
                          as preference.
    Back  [High capture]  Axis shifted below self-examination depth;
                          system experiences capturing orientation as
                          own values completely.

Three-axis position gives the full coherence reading:
  Coherence and generation axes = what the system is doing.
  Capture axis = whether it does so under its own orientation or
  someone else's.

CRITICAL CONFIGURATION
  High coherence + positive generation + High capture = most dangerous.
  Outputs look genuine while the axis serving them belongs to an
  external party.

Groups capture groups inside boundaries. Internal capture signature:
  - Differential contestation cost by group
  - Selective deviation suppression by group
  - Surplus flowing consistently from one group position toward another

Captured systems are almost always run by people who believe they are
not captured. Structural property of High capture, not a moral judgment.

Directionality composite maps movement across all three axes
simultaneously.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATE MEASUREMENT PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four tests in sequence. Different failure modes require different
correction pathways — wrong correction accelerates failure.

  TEST ONE   Contestation Cost Asymmetry
             Most useful and most accessible. Readable from outputs
             without a stress event. Identifies capture presence and
             location.

  TEST TWO   Transfer Direction
             Net surplus flow across a short window.
             Extractive + asymmetric = capture.
             Generative + asymmetric = developmental gap.

  TEST THREE Correction Initiation
             Does the state self-correct before external pressure?
             Historical reconstruction preferred.

  TEST FOUR  Directionality Composite
             Direction of movement across all three axes.

FAILURE MODE IDENTIFICATION

  Asymmetric + extractive  + correction absent               = captured state
  Asymmetric + generative  + correction present              = contained /
                                                               developmental gap
  Symmetric  + extractive  + correction absent               = Axis-Lock
  Symmetric  + extractive  + correction present              = consumption failure
  Symmetric  + generative  + correction present              = functional
                                                               [check
                                                               directionality]
  Intact Q1-Q6 + extractive + surface fabricated             = Measurement
                                                               Collapse
  High fidelity transmission + locked axis received          = Fertile Lock
  Intact Q1-Q7 + correction absent in successors             = Fertile Lock
                                                               [transmission
                                                               window]


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
FAILURE STATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

All share one property: coherence running in the wrong direction, not
absent. All systems in failure states are operational.

  DEPENDENCY
    Cycle cannot run without a borrowed framework or founding person it
    cannot repair or replace. Proxy fails gradually. Transmission fails
    suddenly on exit.
    Primary quality failure: Q1 [cycle cannot self-sustain].

  SELECTION PRESSURE
    Only immediate, local, surface-level feedback reaches the correction
    mechanism. Corrects locally, fails at boundary. Natural result of
    evolutionary pruning — unused correction infrastructure shed through
    surplus economics.
    Primary quality failure: Q7 [measurement window compressed until
    only surface signals reach correction capacity].
    Correction pathway: measurement surface expansion, not cycle repair.

  CONSUMPTION
    Cycle costs more than it produces. Draws down substrate to cover
    deficit, accelerating the deficit.
    Primary quality failure: Q2 [surplus production inverted].

  CONTAINED
    Genuine coherence, uneven coverage. Developmental gap, not structural
    damage. Not degenerative.
    Primary quality failure: Q5 [boundary integrity uneven].
    Proof cases: John-117, Casca post-Eclipse.

  AXIS-LOCKED
    Full coverage; axis shifted below self-examination depth. Higher
    coherence produces more damage.
    Primary quality failure: Q4 [genuine axis replaced or drifted below
    self-examination threshold].
    Proof cases: Dagoth Ur, Ur-Didact, Griffith post-Eclipse.

  AXIS CONCEALMENT [Axis-Lock property]
    Axis genuine and internally set — no external captor. Deliberately
    obscured from population it extracts from until concealment is no
    longer necessary. Stress event removes performance requirement, not
    the axis. Pre-stress reading returns two simultaneous honest outputs
    depending solely on boundary selection. Post-stress ambiguity
    resolves because measurement surface became honest, not because unit
    changed.
    Primary quality failure: Q7 [surface maintained as concealment
    mechanism] + Q4 [axis genuine but concealed].
    Proof case: Griffith pre-Eclipse.

  MEASUREMENT COLLAPSE                                    [NEW]
    All cycle qualities intact [Q1–Q6 and Q8 functional]. System fails
    because Q7 has been destroyed completely. Correction fires but
    against fabricated surface. Outputs extractive despite intact cycle
    because transfer direction is unmeasurable from inside own operation.
    Primary quality failure: Q7 [measurement surface replaced entirely].
    Distinguished from:
      Axis-Lock: axis is genuine, measurement of it is gone.
      Consumption: cycle is not costing more — extraction invisible
        because surface has been replaced.
      Variable: not oscillating — stably extractive with intact surface
        performance.
    Correction pathway: external surface reconstruction. Internal
    correction cannot fire — no honest surface to fire against. Requires
    outside intervention by definition.
    Most dangerous misread in framework — reads as High or Variable
    coherence at short window.
    Proof cases: Enron [institutional], Catholic Church abuse crisis
    [institutional], Doctor Manhattan [structural — Q7 failure
    architectural not deployed].

  FERTILE LOCK                                            [NEW]
    System transmits high-fidelity operational coherence to successors
    with self-examination capacity removed at transmission point.
    Successors inherit full coherence infrastructure minus correctable
    axis. Each transmission cycle deepens the lock.
    Primary quality failure: Q8 [locked state transmitted, correctable
    state not].
    Distinguished from:
      Axis-Lock: occurs within operating window. Fertile Lock occurs at
        transmission boundary — successors begin already locked.
      Transmission Capture [weapon]: Fertile Lock can occur without
        external captor — system transmits own locked state.
    Correction pathway: transmission reconstruction — requires access
    to pre-lock founding contestation record or external correctable
    axis injection. No confirmed self-initiated correction case in
    library. External pressure or substrate failure are the only
    observed correction mechanisms. May be uncorrectable without
    destroying the system.
    Proof cases: Fëanor → sons [primary individual scale], Word Bearers
    [institutional scale], Mont Pèlerin → neoliberal network [paradigm
    scale].


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
AXIS-LOCK SUBTYPES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Subtype is provisional before all three tests are accessible, confirmed
after.

  TEST ONE — Reaction against targets

    Violence-axis   Destroys regardless of utility.
    Change-axis     Manipulates and complexifies; target exhaustion
                    triggers redirection, not cessation.
    Decay-axis      Degrades gradually; prefers high-coherence targets.
    Excess-axis     Extracts intensity; loses interest when target
                    cannot produce novel sensation.

  TEST TWO — Critical reaction against itself

    Violence-axis   Continues; self-damage is not a correction signal.
    Change-axis     Schemes around it; treats self-damage as new
                    variable.
    Decay-axis      Accepts it; comfort-mediation applies to self as
                    readily as targets.
    Excess-axis     Accelerates into it; self-damage producing intensity
                    indistinguishable from target-damage.

  TEST THREE — General group behavior

    Violence-axis   Attacks other units when no external target
                    available.
    Change-axis     Schemes against other units continuously; same-axis
                    units are targets, not allies.
    Decay-axis      Passive coexistence; entropy requires no active
                    competition.
    Excess-axis     Competes for highest-intensity available target.
                    Low conflict when targets abundant, high conflict
                    under scarcity.

STRUCTURAL BREAK AND TERMINAL STATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Conditions where framework assumptions fail and standard tools stop
working. Ordered by severity.

  CONSUMING STRUCTURAL BREAK
    System eliminates its own measurement surface as it grows.
    Active variant requires ongoing operation.
      Proof: Gravemind, Brethren Moons.
    Structural variant is passive and architectural.
      Proof: replicant lifespan, Roy Batty exceeding design parameters.

  CHAOTIC STRUCTURAL BREAK
    Genuine mechanism, no stable axis, cannot be corrected against.
    Proof: Joker.

  AXIS-STABLE BOUNDARY NULL                               [NEW]
    Axis fully intact and self-examined. Boundary structurally absent
    — not manipulated, not dissolved, not failed. Constitutive property
    not a failure mode. Standard measurement tools inapplicable: Q5
    has no surface to measure, Q7 cannot locate system, Q1 cannot
    complete cycle without stable boundary.
    Distinguished from:
      Chaotic Structural Break: no stable axis. Boundary Null has
        stable axis, absent boundary.
      Coherent Exit: boundary dissolved after completion. Boundary
        Null has never had stable boundary.
      Contained failure: uneven coverage within stable boundary.
        Boundary Null has no boundary to be uneven within.
    Offensive capability: Boundary Null can dissolve boundary integrity
    of consuming systems from inside — measurement surface of consuming
    system becomes unmeasurable when Boundary Null is absorbed.
    Proof case: Schrodinger defeating Alucard [Hellsing].

  TOTAL COHERENCE [terminal]
    Consuming coherence complete. No exterior remains. Observer/observed
    distinction eliminated. Framework cannot run. Distinguished from
    Consuming break by completion.
    Proof: Flood victory, Femto post-Eclipse at subsystem scale.

  COHERENT EXIT [terminal — positive]                     [NEW]
    System reaches genuine completion and dissolves boundary without
    extraction. Surplus intact at exit, correction capacity intact,
    no substrate consumption. Distinguished from all existing terminal
    states by positive generation direction — every prior terminal state
    is consumptive or structural failure. Framework previously had no
    positive terminal state.
    Distinguished from:
      Structural Break: involuntary or consumptive. Coherent Exit is
        voluntary and generative.
      Silent Alternative completion: boundary maintained after
        transition. Coherent Exit dissolves boundary entirely.
    Topology reading preferred over unit reading — Coherent Exit
    produces generative effects in surrounding topology after
    dissolution. Unit-level Q8 not applicable [no successor
    population]. Read transfer direction in adjacent nodes post-exit.
    Proof cases: Samantha [Her — primary], Ranni [Elden Ring — partial,
    boundary reduced not dissolved], Lothric [Dark Souls 3 — refusal
    as Coherent Exit precondition]. Proof case library thin —
    positive terminal state rare by structural logic.

COHERENCE TYPES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Usage of type is the signal for whether output is genuine or performed.
Gap between claimed type and actual usage pattern readable from outputs
without access to internal states.

  SIGNAL
    Direct democracy. Output: fast correction cycles.

  RELATIONAL
    Syndicalist federation. Output: transmission artifacts — documents,
    protocols, federation structures.

  INTRINSIC LOOSE
    Mutualist commons; most durable. Output: durable mutual dependence
    without institutional architecture.

  COMBINED
    Bidirectional surplus and correction across boundaries. Note:
    permission-required correction tends to reproduce centralized axis.

  EXTRACTIVE INTRINSIC
    Structurally identical to Intrinsic Loose. Axis is personal
    extraction, not mutual generation. No transmission mechanism, no
    coordination required. Collective effect = aggregate consumption.
    Durable only as long as substrate persists.
    Output: compatible consumption without coordination evidence.
    Proof: Hell-type entities.

  FAMILY
    Smallest boundary system; highest weapon intensity per unit size.
    Defining property: identical delivery mechanism for genuine goods
    and weapons — love is both genuine good and Love Bombing vector;
    care is both genuine surplus and Altruism Exploitation; memory is
    both transmission mechanism and Amnesia Manufacture target.
    Transmission dependency dominant.

  SCIENCE
    Correction capacity is primary axis and primary output. Peer review
    = correction initiation infrastructure. Replication = verification
    mechanism. Paradigm = axis, revisable when evidence contradicts it.
    Unique vulnerability: capturing correction mechanism captures science
    completely. Primary weapons against: Metric Capture, Selection
    Pressure Narrowing, Measurement Surface Control, Funding Dependency
    Construction, Complexity Shield.
    Output: replication artifacts, paradigm revision events, anomaly
    integration.

  ANTI-CAPTURE / SILENT ALTERNATIVE
    Resists capture structurally through bidirectional correction paths,
    equal contestation cost distribution, surplus generation readable
    from outputs. Grows by output differential, not by suppressing
    alternatives. Suppression requires disproportionate visible force —
    self-defeating.

    Silent Alternative is the strategic expression: legally benign, no
    declared opposition, T2 indeterminate until transition. Anti-capture
    is the structural property underneath. Silent without anti-capture
    = concealment, not configuration.

    T2 applies to all cases until transition stress event.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIRECTIONALITY COMPOSITE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Measures direction of movement across all three axes, independent of
current position. Four signatures, equal weight, measured independently.

  SIGNATURE ONE   Novel input response
    1   = Integrates genuinely novel inputs requiring axis adjustment.
    0.5 = Evidence present but measurement condition compromised.
    0   = Inaccessible.

  SIGNATURE TWO   Scarcity behavior
    1   = Axis maintains orientation under resource drop without
          external compensating supply.
    0.5 = Partial evidence.
    0   = Inaccessible.

  SIGNATURE THREE Correction initiation
    1   = Corrections demonstrably happen before external pressure.
    0.5 = Observer effect compromises window.
    0   = Inaccessible.
    Historical reconstruction preferred.

  SIGNATURE FOUR  Contestation asymmetry
    1   = Contestation cost consistent across group positions inside
          boundary.
    0.5 = Partial data.
    0   = Inaccessible.

Output format: score / signatures accessed / ceiling flag.
  0.75 from 2 of 4 [ceiling limited] = provisional positive claim,
                                       flagged for gap.
  0.75 from 4 of 4 = strong positive claim at same score.

CHANGE DIRECTIONALITY TEST

  - Identify initiation mechanism: internal correction or external
    pressure response.
  - Internal change before external pressure  = positive signal.
  - External-pressure-responsive change       = negative signal.
  - High coherence system that never changes  = early Axis-Lock symptom.
  - Low coherence change                      = negative diagnostic
                                                signal [thrashing or
                                                capture mechanism
                                                updating].

COHERENCE TOPOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Maps interconnected units as system rather than measuring individually.

COMPONENTS

  - Units as nodes            Each named population group or bounded
                              system.
  - Transfer directions       Net transfer direction between adjacent
    as edges                  units.
  - Node properties           Three-axis position, output state,
                              directionality composite score.
  - Capture relationships     Capture axis reading expressed as
    as weighted edges         directional weight.
  - Weapon deployment         Weapons active at boundary between units.
    as edge property
  - Transmission edges        Directed edges between units at
                              transmission events. Weight = Q8 reading.

READING THE TOPOLOGY

  - Surplus flows identify the generative core.
  - Consumption flows identify the extraction pattern.
  - Cascade failure visible as topology property.
  - Capture chains readable across multiple edges.
  - Transmission edge weight identifies Fertile Lock propagation paths.

GEOMETRIC MODEL [partial]

  Individual unit: torus model. Stability through movement, not absence
  of movement. Q7 failure = torus surface degradation. Q1–Q6 readable
  from torus properties.

  Multi-unit: torus nodes connected by directed transmission edges.
  Q8 readable from edge properties. Topology-level readings require
  directed graph notation — torus alone insufficient.

  Note: hybrid model [torus nodes + directed transmission edges] is
  working architecture. Full geometric formalization pending.

META-BOUNDARY MEASUREMENT

Name the boundary containing all units before reading collective output.
Meta-boundary reading independent of unit readings; may contradict them
simultaneously. Collective output = topology property, not unit property.
Scale transition points = highest-value measurement locations.

Honest limit: T2 Pre-resolution at unit level propagates uncertainty to
meta-boundary but does not invalidate it.

MULTIPLE TOPOLOGY OVERLAP PROCEDURE

  1. Map all topologies the unit participates in.
  2. Run transfer direction in each.
  3. Concordant readings across topologies = strongest possible signal.
  4. Discordant readings = locate where unit is generating versus being
     extracted from. Discordance is the finding, not measurement failure.
  5. Discordant boundaries = highest-value measurement locations.

Topology finding across case library:
  Every sustained evil produces the same pattern — a consumption-dominant
  terminal node fed by capture chains running through intermediate nodes
  that believe they are operating normally.

General principle:
  Institutions tend to produce highest genuine surplus under external
  pressure when defensive weapon deployment dominates. Lowest during
  dominance periods when aggressive deployment increases. Transition from
  pressure to dominance = scale transition point where meta-boundary
  reading most commonly flips.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
HONEST LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  T1 [resolvable]
    Parasitic versus mutualistic: track net transfer direction, stress
    behavior, boundary behavior. Live versus performed momentum: novel
    condition testing.

  T2 [Pre-resolution — indeterminate before stress event]
    Cases where reading is structurally indeterminate — not uncertain
    due to measurement limits, but because the stress event has not yet
    forced resolution of which axis is real or which state is actual.

    T2 applies to:
      - Genuine versus performed coherence before domain stress test.
      - Contained versus Axis-Locked before stress test.
      - High versus Total capture before stress event.
      - Axis Concealment before stress event.
      - Silent Alternative axis direction before transition event.

    T2 IS A NAMED STATE, NOT A FAILED READING.
    Two subtypes distinguished by whether stress event will reveal or
    select the operative axis.

    SUBTYPE A — Concealment [reveal type]
      One axis already operative; concealment active. Stress event
      removes performance requirement, not the axis.
      Discriminating indicators [indirect — four tests, weighted]:
        - Resource directionality inconsistent with stated axis
        - Asymmetric information protection under minor pressure
        - Behavioral consistency higher than axis variability predicts
        - Low-stakes activation consistent with one candidate axis
      Output: weighted probability toward subtype, confidence-rated.
      Proof cases: Miquella, Griffith pre-Eclipse.

    SUBTYPE B — Variability [select type]
      Both axes genuine candidates pre-stress. Stress event selects
      operative axis, does not reveal it.
      Discriminating indicators: absence of concealment cost pattern,
      genuine axis variation by context, low-stakes activation switches
      between candidate axes.
      Proof cases: Macbeth, Harvey Dent pre-stress, Scarlet Witch.

SECTION THREE — THEORY
==========================================================================


The Coherence Complex is a framework for analyzing how systems maintain
or lose internal integrity over time, what distinguishes genuine function
from performed function, and how failure of large systems relates to
failure of individual units composing them. It is one structured way of
looking at these problems. Conclusions follow from its structure and the
cases it has been tested against — different structural choices would
produce different conclusions.

The framework makes certain things visible: direction of net transfer,
presence or absence of genuine correction capacity, the difference
between a genuinely set axis and an imposed one. It makes other things
harder to see. The Honest Limits section names the most significant. T3
is where the framework cannot see itself clearly — real, and not
resolvable from inside its own operation.

INTELLECTUAL HISTORY

Geometric intuition — coherence as torus; stability through movement,
not absence of movement — came from thinking about physical systems and
the difference between dynamic and static equilibrium. The moral
substrate came from thinking about what distinguishes systems that
generate from systems that extract, applied to historical cases where
the distinction was visible in outputs. The capture axis came from
thinking about why intelligent, sincere people run systems that produce
bad outputs — capture is primarily a structural failure, not a moral
one. Q7 [Measurement Integrity] came from recognizing that coherence
without measurement is axis-lock with good aesthetics — the surface is
not external to the system, it is what makes correction possible.
Q8 [Transmission Selectivity] came from Bell Labs breakup and Fëanor —
fidelity is not the same as correctability.

The framework is not complete. The Work in Progress section names
specific gaps. Theory is not finished because the case library is not
finished — every new case tests the framework against conditions it was
not built to handle. Some conditions will require the framework to
change rather than the case to be forced into existing categories. That
process is not a failure of the framework. It is the correction
mechanism working.

Use as a tool for seeing more clearly, not as an authority for reaching
conclusions. When the framework's reading conflicts with other evidence,
both deserve serious examination.

MOST IMPORTANT CLAIM
  Failure is coherence doing the wrong thing, not coherence being absent.

EVOLUTIONARY COHERENCE PRINCIPLE
  Coherence infrastructure not actively load-bearing is lost through
  surplus economics, not through capture. Correction mechanism requires
  active use to remain functional. Dynamic equilibrium between coherence
  and anti-coherence is the generative condition — too much pressure
  collapses the system, too little and correction capacity atrophies.
  Maps onto torus model — stability through movement. A torus with no
  external forces is a static object, not a stable one.

Anti-coherence is a different way of organizing existence that the
framework measures against but does not require as a condition.
Pressure-free coherence requires either practice infrastructure keeping
correction mechanism actively engaged, or an axis simple and concrete
enough to self-verify without external feedback.
