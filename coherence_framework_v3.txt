COHERENCE FRAMEWORK — SCHEMA v3
================================


SECTION ONE — METHOD
====================

BASIC QUALITIES OF COHERENCE
Six qualities. Mutually dependent — missing one is a specific failure mode at that quality.

Generative cycle — completes a loop, takes in, transforms, returns. Opposite: cycle collapse or performance.
Surplus production — outputs exceed cost of running cycle across honest window. Moral content lives here. Opposite: consumption dominance.
Correction capacity — detects contradiction between outputs and orientation, moves without external pressure. Opposite: correction absence or capture.
Genuine axis — orientation from internal generation with self-examination present. Includes transmission fidelity. Opposite: imposed axis.
Boundary integrity — honest definition of who is inside boundary, does not shift to exclude populations experiencing harm. Opposite: boundary manipulation.
Proportional response — correction force proportional to deviation detected, not to threat correction poses to existing power. Opposite: overcorrection as suppression.

Structural order: generative cycle is precondition. Surplus production is where moral value lives. Correction capacity keeps cycle honest. Genuine axis gives correction direction. Boundary integrity keeps measurement honest. Proportional response keeps correction from becoming next failure.

Anti-coherence runs same order in reverse.


GOALS
Accurate measurement of generating versus consuming across defined population and time window. Named parameters before measurement begins — boundary, population, window, substrate.
Identification of failure mechanism not just failure presence. Each failure state has different correction pathway.
Practical utility. If a component does not produce a reading more useful than intuition alone, it does not belong.


VALUES
Good = material security + reduced coercive exposure + expanded options. Named population groups, defined relational boundary, honest window. Trackable from outputs without access to internal states.
Bad = transfers that increase coercive exposure, reduce material security, or narrow options regardless of intent. Intent = mechanism evidence only, does not modify output reading.
Framework does not initiate critical harm in response to Low coherence. Response to critical harm = minimum necessary, contextual, measured from outputs.


COHERENCE WEAPON TEST
Asks: is this system being used as a weapon against coherence rather than as an expression of it? Applied first in any measurement.

Framework maintains living index of identified coherence weapons. Each weapon carries percentage value reflecting relative damage to coherence, calibrated against case library. Index grows and reduces as weapons are identified, consolidated, or found redundant.

Scoring procedure:
- Identify contextually relevant weapons. Irrelevant weapons exit pool entirely — do not score zero.
- Weapon clearly present = full percentage value.
- Close match = half value.
- Not relevant = exits pool.
- Final score = sum of scored values / sum of relevant pool total values. High percentage bad. Low percentage good. Zero = no weapons present or none relevant — distinction noted in report.

Deployment direction identified before scoring:
- Defensive [protecting genuine coherence against external extraction pressure] = noted in report, not scored against system.
- Aggressive [suppressing measurement of own extraction] = scores normally.
- Direction determined by net transfer reading not stated intent.

Scale and timescale note: large institutions across long timescales expected to show high weapon deployment regardless of coherence level. Diagnostic question is proportion defensive versus aggressive within specific measurement window.

Short window most reliable for weapon detection. High concentration in short recent window = active obscuration of something present not historical.

======================
SECTION TWO — MEASUREMENT
======================

Parameters required before measurement begins: Population, Window, Method, Substrate. Missing any = Variable by default.

Output states: None / Low / Middle / Variable / High / State. Middle requires substrate specification or returns Variable.

Rules:
- Legitimacy and morality = parallel tracks measured separately.
- Intent = mechanism evidence only, does not modify output reading.
- Population groups measured separately, not aggregated.
- Boundary selection = value selection. Moral filter applies before boundary selection.
- Past novel-input responses = most reliable evidence within window.
- Net transfer direction distinguishes parasitic from mutualistic. Volume secondary to direction.
- Individual actor properties = mechanism evidence only. Actor reading and system reading are parallel tracks.


THREE-AXIS MEASUREMENT

Coherence axis [horizontal] — cycle integrity across all six basic qualities. Left = anti-coherence, right = coherence. Independent of moral direction.

Generation axis [vertical] — surplus direction. Up = positive generation for unit and surrounding units. Down = extraction from unit and surrounding units.

Capture axis [depth] — degree to which orientation has been conscripted by external force.
- Front [Low capture] = friction at boundary, correction capacity intact.
- Mid [Mild capture] = selective deviation suppression internalized as preference.
- Back [High capture] = axis shifted below self-examination depth, experiences capturing orientation as own values completely.

Three-axis position gives full coherence reading. Coherence and generation axes describe what the system is doing. Capture axis describes whether it is doing it under its own orientation or someone else's.

High coherence + positive generation + High capture = most dangerous configuration — outputs look genuine while axis serving them belongs to external party.

Groups capture groups inside boundaries. Internal capture signature: differential contestation cost by group, selective deviation suppression by group, surplus flowing from one group position toward another consistently.

Captured systems almost always run by people who believe they are not captured — structural property of High capture not moral judgment.

Directionality composite maps movement across all three axes simultaneously.

STATE MEASUREMENT PROCEDURE [subsection of Three-Axis]
Four tests in sequence. Different failure modes require different correction pathways — wrong correction accelerates failure.

Test one — Contestation Cost Asymmetry. Most useful, most accessible. Readable from outputs without stress event. Identifies capture presence and location.
Test two — Transfer Direction. Net surplus flow across short window. Extractive + asymmetric = capture. Generative + asymmetric = developmental gap.
Test three — Correction Initiation. Does state self-correct before external pressure? Historical reconstruction preferred.
Test four — Directionality Composite. Direction of movement across all three axes.

Failure mode identification:
Asymmetric + extractive + correction absent = captured state.
Asymmetric + generative + correction present = contained state / developmental gap.
Symmetric + extractive + correction absent = Axis-Lock state.
Symmetric + extractive + correction present = consumption failure.
Symmetric + generative + correction present = functional state — check directionality for trajectory.

DIRECTIONALITY COMPOSITE
Measures direction of movement across all three axes independent of current position. Four signatures, equal weight, measured independently.

Signature one — novel input response. 1 = integrates genuinely novel inputs requiring axis adjustment. 0.5 = evidence present but measurement condition compromised. 0 = inaccessible.
Signature two — scarcity behavior. 1 = axis maintains orientation under resource drop without external compensating supply. 0.5 = partial evidence. 0 = inaccessible.
Signature three — correction initiation. 1 = corrections demonstrably happen before external pressure arrives. 0.5 = observer effect compromises window. 0 = inaccessible. Historical reconstruction preferred.
Signature four — contestation asymmetry. 1 = contestation cost consistent across group positions inside boundary. 0.5 = partial data. 0 = inaccessible.

Output format: score / signatures accessed / ceiling flag.
0.75 from two of four [ceiling limited] = provisional positive claim flagged for gap.
0.75 from four of four = strong positive claim at same score.

Change directionality test:
- Identify initiation mechanism — internal correction or external pressure response.
- Internal change before external pressure = positive signal.
- External-pressure-responsive change = negative signal.
- High coherence system that never changes = early Axis-Lock symptom.
- Low coherence change = negative diagnostic signal — thrashing or capture mechanism updating.


COHERENCE TOPOLOGY
Maps interconnected units as a system rather than measuring individually.

Components:
- Units as nodes — each named population group or bounded system.
- Transfer directions as edges — net transfer direction between adjacent units.
- Node properties — three-axis position, output state, directionality composite score.
- Capture relationships as weighted edges — capture axis reading expressed as directional weight.
- Weapon deployment as edge property — weapons active at boundary between units.

Reading the topology:
- Surplus flows identify generative core.
- Consumption flows identify extraction pattern.
- Cascade failure visible as topology property.
- Capture chains readable across multiple edges.

Meta-boundary measurement — name the boundary containing all units before reading collective output. Meta-boundary reading independent of unit readings, may contradict them simultaneously. Collective output = topology property not unit property. Scale transition points [where unit readings flip at meta-boundary level] = highest-value measurement locations.

Honest limit: T2 indeterminate at unit level propagates uncertainty to meta-boundary but does not invalidate it.

Multiple Topology Overlap Procedure:
- Map all topologies unit participates in.
- Run transfer direction in each.
- Concordant readings across topologies = strongest possible signal.
- Discordant readings = locate where unit is generating versus being extracted from. Discordance is the finding not measurement failure.
- Discordant boundaries = highest-value measurement locations.

Topology finding across case library: every sustained evil produces identical pattern — consumption-dominant terminal node fed by capture chains running through intermediate nodes that believe they are operating normally.

General principle: institutions tend to produce highest genuine surplus under external pressure when defensive weapon deployment dominates, lowest during dominance periods when aggressive deployment increases. Transition from pressure to dominance = scale transition point where meta-boundary reading most commonly flips.

Not a calibration or data access problem — structural. Framework's measurement tools and weapons against measurement are pointed at the same target from opposite directions.

Legacy Coherence — drawing on genuine past High coherence without current surplus generation. Short window reads Low, long window reads High. Gap between readings is the signal.

Synchronous Contradictory States — unit running genuinely contradictory states simultaneously. Strategic variant: resolves at transition stress event. Structural variant: both sub-units running real axes, damage emerges from contradiction pattern. Real-world analog: severe internal conflict states. Run separate readings per sub-unit. Unified reading T2 indeterminate, per sub-unit T1 resolvable. Proof: Marika/Radagon [structural], Griffith [strategic].


======================
SECTION THREE — THEORY
======================

The Coherence Complex is a framework for analyzing how systems maintain or lose internal integrity over time, what distinguishes genuine function from performed function, and how failure of large systems relates to failure of individual units composing them. It is one structured way of looking at these problems. Conclusions follow from its structure and the cases it has been tested against — different structural choices would produce different conclusions.

The framework makes certain things visible — direction of net transfer, presence or absence of genuine correction capacity, difference between genuinely set axis and imposed one. It makes other things harder to see. Honest limits section names the most significant. T3 limit is where the framework cannot see itself clearly — real and not resolvable from inside its own operation.

Intellectual history: geometric intuition — coherence as torus, stability through movement not absence of movement — came from thinking about physical systems and the difference between dynamic and static equilibrium. Moral substrate came from thinking about what distinguishes systems that generate from systems that extract, applied to historical cases where distinction was visible in outputs. Capture axis came from thinking about why intelligent, sincere people run systems that produce bad outputs — capture is primarily a structural failure not a moral one.

The framework is not complete. Work in progress section names specific gaps. Theory is not finished because case library is not finished — every new case tests framework against conditions it was not built to handle. Some conditions will require the framework to change rather than the case to be forced into existing categories. That process is not a failure of the framework. It is the correction mechanism working.

Use as a tool for seeing more clearly, not as an authority for reaching conclusions. When framework's reading conflicts with other evidence, both deserve serious examination.

Most important claim: failure is coherence doing the wrong thing, not coherence being absent.

Evolutionary coherence principle — coherence infrastructure not actively load-bearing is lost through surplus economics not through capture. Correction mechanism requires active use to remain functional. Dynamic equilibrium between coherence and anti-coherence is the generative condition — too much pressure collapses the system, too little and correction capacity atrophies. Maps onto torus model — stability through movement. A torus with no external forces is a static object, not a stable one.

Anti-coherence is a different way of organizing existence that the framework measures against but does not require as a condition. Pressure-free coherence requires either practice infrastructure keeping correction mechanism actively engaged, or an axis simple and concrete enough to self-verify without external feedback.
