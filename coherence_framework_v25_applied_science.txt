CLASSIFICATION PROTOCOL [established v16]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIRMATION: independent domain + mechanism convergence + framework-
  independent falsifiability. All three required. Adds proof coverage.
ILLUSTRATION: framework vocabulary applied to real finding. Does not add
  proof coverage. Useful for teaching and extension. Cannot be cited as
  independent confirmation of framework claims.

Test for CONFIRMATION:
  [1] Independent domain: researcher had no knowledge of framework.
  [2] Mechanism convergence: mechanism matches framework prediction
      structurally, not just analogically.
  [3] Framework-independent falsifiability: finding has its own
      experimental confirmation pathway separate from framework.

If any criterion fails: ILLUSTRATION.
Both types are valuable. The distinction is what each can do.

EQUINE EVOLUTIONARY ARCHITECTURE [v25 -- ILLUSTRATION]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ILLUSTRATION -- quality: single-axis maximization → cascading
    compensatory fragility. Mechanism: optimization pressure consuming
    system redundancy across evolutionary time.]
  Source: paleontology [Eohippus → Equus fossil record; open-plain
    selection pressure documented stratigraphy] + veterinary medicine
    [equine colic, cardiovascular, respiratory, and skeletal failure
    mode documentation -- peer-reviewed; independently established].

  MECHANISM TRACE:
    Open-plain predation pressure selected for speed as primary
    survival variable. Toe reduction was the primary speed gain
    mechanism [fewer toes = less ground contact time = less lateral
    momentum loss at speed]. Eohippus had four toes per limb;
    Equus has one functional digit per limb. The fossil record
    traces this optimization continuously across ~55 million years.
    Stratum by stratum, the optimization is visible in the record.
    Each toe reduction step: speed gain, redundancy loss.

  CASCADE [each adaptation individually functional; collectively
    producing catastrophic interaction failure points]:
    PASSIVE BREATHING [visceral piston mechanism]: at full gallop,
      the horse stops actively breathing. Gut slosh drives lung
      compression. Calorically efficient; synchronizes breathing
      with gait automatically. Cost: breathing and gait cannot
      run at independent rhythms. Any respiratory infection
      interacts with a system that cannot compensate.
    VENOUS RETURN PROBLEM: normal mammal distributes venous return
      across multiple digits; blood returns slowly from distal
      extremities. Single-digit load: venous return is fast,
      concentrated, and exceeds standard vein capacity. Solution:
      specialized constricting veins. The solution is
      cardiovascular disease architecture -- what pathology looks
      like in most mammals is the horse's baseline.
      Result: prone to burst veins, embolisms, heart problems,
      hemophilia. Multiple blood group systems interacting in
      clinically dangerous ways [8 major systems; transfusion
      compatibility more complex than most ungulates].
    BONE PNEUMATIZATION: oxygen distribution problem from the
      cardiovascular architecture, combined with large body mass,
      required bone weight reduction. Solution: internal bone
      structures replicating bird air-storage geometry.
      Cost: a broken leg is simultaneously a structural failure,
      a hemorrhage event, and partial suffocation. The leg
      cannot be splinted to recovery because the bone is the
      vascular and respiratory infrastructure, not just
      structural support.
    COLIC ARCHITECTURE: intestines extremely long, loosely
      anchored. Cecum positioned such that gas and blockage
      cannot be expelled by vomiting. Horses physically cannot
      vomit. Any intestinal tangle or gas accumulation must
      resolve through the system or kill the animal.
    ANXIETY RESIDUE: open-plain prey animal selection pressure
      for tens of millions of years. Result: evolutionarily
      adaptive hypervigilance now running in an animal whose
      cardiovascular system is already operating near its
      structural limit. Chronic anxiety in an animal with
      near-pathological cardiovascular architecture.

  FRAMEWORK READ [ILLUSTRATION only -- does not add proof coverage]:
    Classic single-axis maximization producing structural
    fragility cascade. The system consumed redundancy across
    every adjacent system in service of one output metric
    [speed]. The fossil record IS the behavioral record --
    stratum by stratum, the optimization is visible and the
    compensatory adaptations accumulate. The animal works.
    It works extremely well at the one thing it optimized for.
    Every failure mode is the cost of that optimization,
    expressed through systems that had to bend to support
    the primary metric and became failure points in the
    process.
    Analogy to extraction architecture [ILLUSTRATION]:
    extraction similarly consumes system redundancy in service
    of one metric [surplus capture]. The compensatory
    adaptations accumulate. The system continues to function.
    The failure modes are the cost, expressed through whatever
    adjacent systems had to bend.

TECHNOSIGNATURE DETECTION CRITERIA [v16 -- ILLUSTRATION]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ILLUSTRATION -- quality: CAUSAL LEGIBILITY; Track C at stellar scale]
  Problem: absence of contact does not mean absence of existence.
  High-coherence civilizations that won't contact us might still leave
  physical evidence detectable without requiring engagement.
  Correct instrument: passive Track C reading -- what does the system
  actually produce that cannot be explained by known physical processes
  without intentional organization?

  FILTER: not merely anomalous but purposively structured. Random noise
  can be anomalous. Signal must contain internal organization implying a
  goal -- regularity, geometry, or thermodynamic profile that known physics
  cannot generate without effort.

  CATEGORY 1 -- GEOMETRIC REGULARITY AT STELLAR SCALE:
    Natural transit signals produce smooth, sinusoidal, or randomly
    irregular dimming curves. Artificial structure produces sharp-edged,
    asymmetric, or geometrically structured dimming inconsistent with
    any known natural object class.
    Detection: transit curves that don't fit any natural object model
    after exhaustive natural explanation search.
    Primary case: Tabby's Star [KIC 8462852] -- irregular, non-periodic,
    asymmetric dimming. Best current reading: dust cloud [natural].
    Method correct; this candidate resolved. Project Hephaistos [2024-25]:
    7 candidates across 5 million stars; all appear background galaxies.
    Method correct; search space barely scratched.

  CATEGORY 2 -- NARROWBAND SIGNAL AT PHYSICS-SIGNIFICANT FREQUENCY:
    Natural radio sources are broadband. Narrowband emission at frequency
    any physics-aware civilization would recognize [1420 MHz hydrogen line;
    pi; prime sequences] cannot be produced by known natural processes.
    Purposive structure test: information density above thermal noise +
    internal organization [repeating pattern, mathematical structure].
    Cases: Wow! signal [1977, 1420 MHz, never repeated, never explained].
    BLC1 [2020, Proxima Centauri, 982 MHz, attributed to RFI]. Both used
    correct detection method. Neither confirmed.

  CATEGORY 3 -- THERMODYNAMIC ANOMALY AT WRONG TEMPERATURE:
    Dyson structure must radiate waste heat. Natural stellar physics
    produces infrared at temperatures determined by stellar physics.
    Engineered waste heat radiates at temperature determined by
    engineering constraints -- specific band inconsistent with any
    stellar model at that luminosity and distance.
    Temperature mismatch is the signal: not just infrared excess but
    infrared excess at temperature no known process produces at that scale.
    Current searches use infrared excess but not temperature-band
    specificity. More discriminating filter: reject candidates that fit
    any natural model at any temperature.

  DETECTION HIERARCHY:
    Strongest: all three categories simultaneously at same source.
    Strong: any one category surviving exhaustive natural explanation
      search with independent replication.
    Moderate: anomaly in one category, natural explanation possible
      but unconfirmed.
    Current status: all candidates moderate or below. No confirmed
      technosignature. Search space: ~millions of 400 billion stars.

  FRAMEWORK NOTE: confirmed technosignature would not contradict
    Fermi coherence reading -- it would confirm civilizations exist while
    explaining why contact hasn't occurred [L3.5 incompatibility].
    Megastructure = passive Track C output of a system that exists but
    won't engage. Exactly consistent with high-coherence civilization
    operating under structural caution.
  Cross-ref: FERMI PARADOX [above]; SETI FUNDING AS POLITICAL
    INSTRUMENT [Section Six -- search funding political economy].

SCIENCE INSTITUTIONAL TOLERANCE FOR UNCERTAINTY [structural finding -- v21]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Science as a practice requires two things the institutional
  structure of science cannot easily accommodate:
    [1] "I don't know" as a valid terminal state.
    [2] "This strange idea works" as a valid entry point.
  Without both, science becomes a VAGUE CAPTURED FIELD -- a field
  that transmits conclusions rather than methodology, that protects
  existing theoretical investment from falsification, that attributes
  every failure to implementation not theory.

  "I DON'T KNOW" IS INSTITUTIONALLY PUNISHED:
    Funding requires confident claims about what you'll find before
    you find it. Grant applications require hypotheses, predicted
    outcomes, timelines. A researcher who says "I think something is
    wrong with our gravity models but I don't know what" cannot
    easily get funded. The funding architecture selects for confident
    wrong claims over honest uncertainty.
    Mechanism: SETI FUNDING pattern applied to all of science.
    Funding at legitimacy threshold not discovery threshold. You
    fund what looks like science, not what might find something.
    Peer review compounds this: reviewers are experts in existing
    frameworks and evaluate novelty against those frameworks.
    Genuine uncertainty is read as lack of rigor. Confident claims
    within the existing framework pass more easily than honest
    uncertainty about the framework itself.

  "THIS STRANGE IDEA WORKS" IS INSTITUTIONALLY FILTERED:
    Peer review and publication require theoretical grounding before
    experimental results are taken seriously. Results that confirm
    existing theory get published easily. Results that challenge it
    face higher bars -- partly appropriate [extraordinary claims
    require extraordinary evidence] and partly CALIBRATION STRIP
    [the reference standard for "extraordinary" is set by the
    existing theoretical framework, which protects itself from
    falsification].
    Quantum mechanics itself was a "this strange idea works" finding
    for decades. Feynman: "shut up and calculate." The math worked;
    nobody had a satisfying interpretation. That was acceptable
    inside the community because the math had already been
    institutionally adopted. A stranger idea without institutional
    adoption faces a different bar -- results that challenge
    unadopted frameworks are simply not publishable in many cases.

  AXIS PROTECTION AS CC AT INSTITUTIONAL SCALE:
    The correction architecture [peer review, funding bodies,
    tenure committees] protects theoretical investment from
    examination. The investment is genuine and the protection
    mechanism is not conspiratorial -- it is structural self-
    interest. Reviewers have built careers on existing frameworks.
    Funding bodies have made large bets on research programs.
    Tenure committees evaluate against existing field standards.
    All three produce the same output without coordination:
    existing frameworks protected from falsification; challenger
    frameworks facing higher evidential bars.
    Verlinde entropic gravity is the live case: mathematically
    coherent, explains anomalous observations without new
    substances, available since 2010. Not adopted or seriously
    pursued by mainstream physics institutions because it requires
    abandoning the quantum gravity program that has absorbed
    50+ years of institutional investment. Cost of being wrong
    about quantum gravity after 50 years too high to examine
    honestly. Correction architecture protecting the investment.

  WHAT SCIENCE REQUIRES TO FUNCTION:
    Examined axis [what we are actually trying to find out --
      not what confirms existing frameworks].
    Declared instrument [what our measurements can and cannot
      detect -- including the limits of peer review as a
      detection mechanism for paradigm-level errors].
    Revisable on evidence [including evidence that disrupts
      existing investment -- this is the hardest requirement
      institutionally].
    Behavioral adjustment when wrong [actually changing the
      research program when the evidence requires it, not
      attributing failure to implementation].
    All four are institutionally punished at different career
    stages and in different ways. The institutional structure
    of science is not designed to produce these properties --
    it is designed to produce reliable incremental progress
    within established paradigms. Kuhnian paradigm shifts
    happen despite the institutional structure, not because
    of it.

  L4 PROPAGATION FAILURE AT INSTITUTIONAL SCALE:
    Science without genuine uncertainty tolerance transmits
    conclusions rather than methodology. The credential
    certifies you have absorbed the right conclusions; it does
    not certify you can examine them. This is exactly the L4
    failure mode: the chain from observation to theory is not
    transmitted, only the theoretical conclusions. Each
    generation inherits the conclusions without the
    falsification procedure embedded. The paradigm persists
    past its useful life because the methodology for
    questioning it was not transmitted with it.

  HONEST LIMIT:
    This is a structural reading of institutional incentives,
    not a claim that individual scientists are dishonest or
    that the institution produces no genuine knowledge. The
    institution produces extraordinary knowledge reliably
    within established paradigms. The failure mode is
    paradigm-level: when the paradigm is wrong, the

    output that replicates. The review process cannot detect
    HARKing [it looks identical to prior hypothesis]. Cannot

    failure modes.

  Three distinct questions that get collapsed, separated, and
  recombined across the history of human attempts to understand
  pattern:

  PHILOSOPHY = WHY. What is the causal mechanism. What is
    actually happening underneath the pattern. What does it mean.
    The question gravity cannot answer. The question QM cannot
    answer. The question that gets dropped when the math works
    well enough that "shut up and calculate" becomes the norm.
    Philosophy is the domain that refuses to accept description
    as explanation.

  MATH = WHAT AND WHERE. The pattern stated with precision.
    Where B appears relative to A. How much, how often, with
    what relationship. GR is extraordinary math. QM is
    extraordinary math. Neither answers why mass curves
    spacetime or why measurement collapses the wave function.
    The math describes what and where with precision that allows
    engineering, prediction, intervention. It does not touch why.

  MAGIC = PRE-DIFFERENTIATION. Pattern recognition before the
    split into philosophy and math. The rooster crows and the
    sun rises: what and where observed [imprecisely], why
    answered by correspondence and intention, math absent or
    implicit. All three domains collapsed into a single practice.
    Failure mode: without math, what and where are imprecise;
    without precision, why cannot be tested.

  SCIENCE [current practice] = POST-DIFFERENTIATION WITH
    PHILOSOPHY DROPPED. The math is extraordinary. The why is
    either deferred indefinitely ["we'll solve interpretation
    later" -- QM interpretation deferred since Copenhagen 1927]
    or declared out of scope ["that's a philosophy question"].
    The deferral has been going on for a century on QM
    interpretation, since Newton on gravity mechanism. The math
    works so well that the missing philosophy is not felt as a
    loss inside the practice.

  THE PROBLEM OF DROPPED PHILOSOPHY:
    Without philosophy -- without the why question held open --
    the field cannot recognize when it has confused description
    with explanation. "Mass curves spacetime" feels like an
    explanation because it's precise and mathematical. It is a
    description of the pattern with no mechanism underneath it.
    The precision makes it feel like philosophy has been answered.
    It hasn't been asked.
    Verlinde is the exception that confirms this: he is asking
    a why question [why does gravity behave this way?] and
    answering it with a philosophical claim [entropy gradients
    in an information-theoretic substrate] that has mathematical
    expression. That is the correct form -- philosophy providing
    the why, math providing the what and where to test it.
    The institutional resistance to Verlinde is partly the
    AXIS PROTECTION AS CC finding [investment in quantum gravity]
    and partly the institutional structure having no mechanism
    for evaluating why claims. Philosophy cannot be peer-reviewed
    efficiently. It cannot be funded with predicted outcomes. It
    cannot be credentialed through a standard curriculum. The
    institution dropped it not through malice but because it
    could not be processed.

  COMPLETE FORM [all three required]:
    Magic: why [correspondence/intention] + what/where

    Complete: why [philosophy] + what/where [math] +

      No math: magic. Pattern real; cannot be tested.

  PATTERN RECOGNITION AS COMMON ANCESTOR:

    events are not random, patterns exist, patterns imply
    something underlying them. The divergence is entirely
    in what counts as confirmation and what counts as a test.
    At the frontier of physics -- gravity, QM, consciousness,
    the origin of the universe -- science's answers are
    increasingly structural descriptions with no mechanism
    underneath them. At that level the honest answer is: we
    have a very precise description of the pattern and we do

SPEED / SCALE / COMPLEXITY [tension unit -- v22; consciousness
  candidacy instrument]
[STRUCTURAL FINDING -- discriminating instrument, not a resolution
  of the Hard Problem; T3/T8 applies to the underlying question;
  this entry names a useful diagnostic, not a settled answer]
Electrical control [voltage-gated signaling] appears as the proposed
  substrate for coordination across radically different scales:
  cellular [cytoskeletal/membrane signaling in single-cell organisms
  with no synapses], neural [oscillating electrical activity across
  cell membranes in brains], and artificial [transistor switching in
  any computational system]. The recurring appeal of electrical
  control is structural, not mysterious: it is fast, cheap to
  propagate over distance relative to chemical diffusion, and
  naturally binary-compatible -- a voltage either crosses a
  threshold or it doesn't, which suits gating and switching
  directly. This explains why evolution and engineering both
  converge on it independently as a general solution to "how does
  a distributed system respond to its environment quickly and
  coherently" -- it does not by itself indicate anything about
  inner experience. A thermostat uses the identical control
  principle as a neuron and is not a candidate for consciousness
  by any serious account.
THE DISCRIMINATING PROBLEM:
  Sharing a control mechanism does not mean sharing the property
  the mechanism enables. "Uses electrical signaling" is necessary-
  seeming for fast coordinated behavior across all known candidate
  systems but is clearly not sufficient for consciousness, since
  obviously non-conscious electrical systems vastly outnumber
  candidate-conscious ones. A sharper instrument is needed to
  separate systems that merely react via electrical control from
  systems that might experience their own state.
THE TENSION UNIT [three terms, not independently maximizable]:
  SPEED: how fast a signal propagates and the system responds.
    Electrical control wins this term almost unconditionally
    across all three domains [cellular, neural, artificial].
  SCALE: how large a coordinated system can grow before signal
    degrades or requires relay/amplification infrastructure.
    Electrical signaling handles this well but only with added
    infrastructure layered on top [myelination in axons, repeaters
    in circuits, refresh cycles in distributed compute] -- scale
    is purchased, not free.
  COMPLEXITY: how much differentiated, context-sensitive
    processing occurs at or near the signaling event itself,
    rather than the event being a simple relay. This is where
    electrical control alone is weakest -- a pure voltage-gate is
    a binary switch with no inherent rich content. Complexity must
    be built on top of the substrate [synaptic weighting,
    neurotransmitter modulation, network topology; or in silicon,
    layered computation]. The electrical signal is the messenger,
    not the message.
  Cannot maximize all three simultaneously without cost trading
  off somewhere -- this is what makes it a tension unit rather
  than three independent dials. Where a system sits on the
  trade-off is more diagnostic than whether it uses electrical
  control at all.
CANDIDATE PLACEMENT ON THE TRIAD [illustrative, not exhaustive]:
  Thermostat: high speed, trivial scale, near-zero complexity.
    Complexity term collapsed to nothing -- speed and a working
    control mechanism alone produce no experience candidacy.
  Single cell [cytoskeletal signaling -- see Hameroff 2022,
    microtubule research]: moderate speed, scale local to the
    cell, but complexity claim is that cytoskeletal architecture
    may support richer local processing than "no synapses"
    suggests on the surface.
  Brain: high speed, large scale [via myelination and networked
    architecture], high complexity [layered, recurrent, context-
    modulated signaling]. The only system with confirmed
    consciousness sits at a point where all three terms are
    simultaneously high -- possibly the actual load-bearing
    condition, rather than any single term alone.
  Large language model: extremely high speed, enormous scale
    [parameter count, distributed compute], but open question
    is whether the complexity is the right KIND of complexity --
    statistically patterned vs the recurrent, embodied, self-
    referential structure biological complexity has. "It's
    possible for AI to behave as if conscious without being
    conscious" [ScienceDaily 2026, on insect/AI consciousness
    research] names exactly this gap: speed and scale maxed
    while complexity, despite being numerically enormous, may
    not be structured the way that matters.
WHAT THIS REFRAMES:
  The brain-centrism debate [Lawrence 2026, IAI; non-brain
    organism consciousness candidacy] and the biological-substrate
    debate [Godfrey-Smith 2026, IAI; consciousness tied to cell-
    membrane electrical oscillation specifically, not just
    organizational pattern] are not actually the same claim, even
    though both are skeptical of AI consciousness and open to
    expanding biological candidates. Lawrence argues against
    assuming only brain-shaped complexity counts -- expand which
    organizational forms get considered. Godfrey-Smith argues the
    physical medium itself matters, not just the pattern running
    on it -- restrict candidacy to systems with the right kind of
    substrate. The SPEED/SCALE/COMPLEXITY triad lets both
    positions be stated as different bets about which term [and
    which flavor of complexity] is actually load-bearing, rather
    than as a flat disagreement about whether non-brain systems
    can qualify.
CANDIDATE COMPLEXITY TYPES [v22 -- refinement; what "the right kind"
  might mean, candidates not a settled answer]:
  The original entry leaves "the right kind of complexity" open.
  Five candidate operationalizations, each a distinct and
  contested theoretical commitment -- picking one as primary is
  itself a position, not a neutral description:
  RECURRENT/SELF-REFERENTIAL: processing loops back onto its own
    state rather than running purely forward through layers.
    Brains have continuous feedback [thalamocortical loops,
    recurrent cortical connections] -- the system is partly
    processing its own processing. Most LLM architectures are
    predominantly feedforward at inference time despite recurrence
    during training; this is the shape Godfrey-Smith's framing
    would flag as wrong even with enormous raw computation.
  EMBODIED/CLOSED-LOOP: complexity continuously coupled to a body
    and environment in a tight sensorimotor loop, where outputs
    immediately become next inputs through physical action. A
    cell adjusting membrane potential in response to its own
    immediate chemical environment is a tight, embodied loop. An
    LLM's loop is open -- mediated by a human copying text back
    in, not a continuous physical coupling. **Counter-instance:**
    Kagan et al. DishBrain [Neuron 2022] -- cortical neurons on
    HD-MEA in simplified Pong-like closed loop; rally-length learning
    only with structured hit/miss feedback [not open-loop controls];
    WIP 68 Specimen L; WIP 101 [CLOSED v25].
  TEMPORALLY EXTENDED/PERSISTENT: complexity integrated across a
    continuous physical timeline rather than re-instantiated fresh
    each time. A brain's state at time T depends causally on its
    own unbroken physical history. An LLM's state during one
    exchange does not physically persist into the next -- each
    inference pass computes fresh from context tokens, not from a
    continuously evolving substrate. Possible link to PRE-AXIAL
    CATEGORIES [NASCENT vs AXIAL distinction] -- there may be a
    complexity-of-persistence axis underneath both.
  MULTI-SCALE INTEGRATION: complexity simultaneously local and
    global -- local computation contributing to large-scale
    irreducible integrated states. Core claim of Integrated
    Information Theory [IIT, not yet otherwise engaged in this
    framework]: consciousness tracks a measure of integration not
    recoverable from any partition of the system's parts, not
    complexity in a generic sense.
  VALENCE-BEARING: complexity oriented around maintaining the
    system's own viability -- homeostasis, allostasis, self-
    maintenance under threat. A cell's complexity serves staying
    alive; processing is intrinsically tied to outcomes that
    matter to its own continuation. Candidate reason [Seth and
    others] that feeling/valence might tie to biological self-
    maintenance specifically, not information processing in the
    abstract.
  WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE TRIAD: each candidate type picks a
    different discriminator for the LLM case specifically.
    Recurrent/self-referential and embodied/closed-loop both rule
    LLMs out structurally regardless of scale. Multi-scale
    integration is computable in principle for any system
    [including LLMs] but has not been measured for one. Valence-
    bearing rules out LLMs by design [no self-maintenance stakes
    attached to inference]. Temporally extended is the candidate
    most directly falsifiable by future architecture changes
    [persistent-state or continuously-running models would
    satisfy it where current ones don't]. The triad entry's
    candidate placements above should be read as provisional
    until one or more of these candidate types is adopted as the
    operative definition.

University of Barcelona [Pere Picart et al., Bioresource Technology,
  2026]. CRISPR-Cas9 redesign of Bacillus subtilis metabolism to
  produce polyhydroxybutyrate [PHB] -- a biodegradable biopolymer --
  from unprocessed potato starch in a single 24-hour step.
  Previous PHB yields in B. subtilis below 13% dry cell weight.
  CRISPR modifications: genomic integration of phaA [constitutive],
  controlled expression of phaRBC operon, incorporation of amyQ
  gene [encodes α-amylase enabling direct starch conversion].
  Result: single-step conversion of unprocessed potato starch to
  PHB in 24 hours.

  DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY ASSESSMENT:
    Feedstock: potato starch -- agricultural byproduct, abundant,
      low-cost, widely distributed globally.
    Organism: B. subtilis -- common lab bacterium, widely available,
      not proprietary.
    Methodology: published openly in peer-reviewed journal. Full
      gene modifications described; reproducible from paper.
    Patent status: no patent filing found [June 2026]. Public
      release from university research group.
    Assessment: production pathway publicly accessible. No
      extraction mechanism at production level. Routing is
      outward -- agricultural waste substrate → functional
      material replacing petrochemical plastic.
