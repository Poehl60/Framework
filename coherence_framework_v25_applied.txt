PRIMARY DEFINITION:
  Politics is the contested control of routing boundaries -- who sets
  them, who they serve, who can see them, and who absorbs the cost of
  their placement. Legitimacy surface required because boundary control
  at scale needs consent or ignorance from cost-absorbers.
  Administration = implementing agreed routing. Predation = honest
  boundary violation without legitimacy surface. Politics = contested
  routing boundary control with legitimacy surface required.
  Cross-ref: EXTRACTION [operational definition]; BOUNDARY CONTROL
    [FRAMEWORK PURPOSE]; AXIS PROTECTION AS CC [deepest resistance
    point -- axis determines what counts as legitimate routing].

POLITICS / RELIGION / GOVERNMENT [structural comparison -- v22]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  T3 Medium declared. Analyst prior active [anti-extraction frame;
  Anti-extraction constitutionalist position]. Apply caution to readings
  that confirm prior without adversarial check.

  SHARED STRUCTURE [all three]:
    All three are routing boundary control systems at civilizational
    scale. All require a legitimacy surface to operate -- raw power
    without legitimacy surface is predation, not any of these three.
    All determine:
      BI [who is inside the boundary].
      MI [who controls the measurement surface].
      SP → SD [where surplus flows].
      CC [what counts as correction].
      TS [what gets transmitted across generations].
    All are high-value weapon targets for the same reason they are
    high-value coherence vehicles: capturing routing boundary control
    at civilizational scale captures everything downstream.
    All are L4.5 candidates -- the only systems in the case library
    that reliably transmit methodology across generations at
    civilizational scale [when functioning correctly].

  RELIGION:
    Primary axis: VERTICAL [individual instrument ↔ higher coherence
      or truth axis]. The routing boundary includes the dead, the
      unborn, the cosmological. Not bounded by territory or living
      population.
    Time horizon: civilizational to cosmological. Longest of the three.
    CC mechanism: conscience, confession, prayer, examination of
      conduct -- INTERNAL correction before external pressure. S3
      signal [correction fires before external pressure arrives] when
      functioning correctly. The correction fires against the self,
      not primarily against the external world.
    Legitimacy source: the idea [high coherence; genuine axis; outward
      SP] as distinct from the institution. GOD/INSTITUTION COHERENCE
      GAP -- the idea consistently outperforms its institutional
      transmission. The institution is a TS vessel that typically fails
      the idea at the scale transition.
    Primary strength: vertical axis contact + internal CC [conscience
      fires against self without requiring external coordination].
    Primary weapon vulnerability:
      GA capture at formation window [doctrine installs axis before
        examination possible; Formation Capture during Nascent window].
      Coherence Rent [institution extracting rent from idea's
        legitimacy surface without contributing to the axis].
      Mythic Formation Capture [genuine cultural substrate deployed
        as state legitimacy surface -- hardest variant to counter].

  POLITICS:
    Primary axis: HORIZONTAL [instruments ↔ each other; power
      distribution across living population within territory].
    Time horizon: electoral cycle to generational. Shorter than
      religion; longer than administrative budget cycle.
    CC mechanism: elections, protest, legal challenge, institutional
      check -- EXTERNAL correction requiring collective coordination.
      The correction requires collective action, not individual
      conscience. Slow, coordination-dependent, capturable at
      coordination layer.
    Legitimacy source: consent of governed [real or manufactured].
      Manufactured consent = MSC at population scale [the measurement
      surface tells the population they consented to what they didn't
      examine].
    Primary strength: horizontal CC [distributed correction capacity
      across population; harder to fully capture than vertical axis].
    Primary weapon vulnerability:
      MSC at population scale [captured measurement surface].
      Proxy Construction [route CC toward decoy targets; actual
        routing boundary control protected].
      Calibration Strip [redefine who counts as the population;
        shift BI before MI captures].
      Diagnostic Collapse [correction fires too late; substrate
        cannot absorb it].
    Cross-ref: order_truth ANALYST AXIS DOCTRINE [democracy-costume demon habitat;
      disorder-middle -- pointer].

  GOVERNMENT:
    Primary axis: ADMINISTRATIVE [procedure → outcome; law →
      enforcement]. Jurisdictional boundary. Not primarily about
      ideas [religion] or power contest [politics] -- about
      procedure execution.
    Time horizon: budget cycle to constitutional. Shortest of three
      for operational decisions; longest for structural ones [constitution].
    CC mechanism: law, enforcement, judicial review -- PROCEDURAL
      correction with institutional backing and force monopoly.
      Requires authority, not just conscience or coordination.
      The correction pathway is nominally present in all governments;
      practically accessible only where power asymmetry permits.
    Legitimacy source: legal mandate [real or captured] + force
      monopoly [Weber's definition: state as entity with monopoly
      on legitimate use of violence within territory].
    Primary strength: force monopoly [correction can be enforced,
      not just advocated] + procedural legitimacy [correction
      pathway formally defined and theoretically accessible].
    Primary weapon vulnerability:
      Regulatory Capture [CC mechanism captured by the population
        it was supposed to correct -- the regulator serves the
        regulated].
      Discovery Weaponization [correction pathway nominally present;
        practically inaccessible due to resource asymmetry].
      Correction Theatre [enforcement activity without enforcement
        output; visible correction process; no target changes].

    Religion's CC fires internally [conscience; before external

    Politics = the contest for routing boundary control.
    Government = the administration of the routing boundary

      contest = extraction without politics. Administration
      of the routing boundary in the interest of the
      capturing actor. Normal state, not exception.
    Distinction: politics requires legitimacy surface to be
      contested. Government requires legitimacy surface to
      be accepted. When acceptance is manufactured: politics
      ends; administration of extraction begins.

      POLITICAL FINDINGS -- v25 standard use].

ROUTING BOUNDARY MONOPOLY [structural finding -- v21]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The problem in union busting, corporate concentration, and nation-
  state power accumulation is structurally identical: routing boundary
  monopoly at a given scale. The intervention logic follows from this.

    correction pathway that could fire against it. Intervention: restore
    distributed CC at employment scale. Unions add a correction node;
    they don't eliminate one.

  CORPORATE CONCENTRATION: routing control monopolized at economic
    scale. Employer/capital surplus routing controlled by fewer actors;
    market correction disabled. Intervention: prevent monopoly formation,
    maintain genuine competition [Ordoliberal]. Corp busting [state
    elimination of corporations] is not equivalent to union formation --
    see below.

    routing monopoly -- competition theater and slice rivalry, not

  NATION AS SOLE CENTRAL AUTHORITY [the error in "corp busting"]:
    Nations busting corporations to become sole routing authority does
    not solve extraction -- it eliminates competition for the extraction
    position and makes the state the uncontested extractor. State-owned
    economies do not produce distribution; they produce state extraction
    without the friction of competing extractors. History confirms:
    consistent reproduction of extraction architecture with state as
    the node, surplus routing upward unchanged. The four-condition test
    fails identically.
    The parallel does not run in both directions:
      Unions are good because they ADD a distributed correction node
        at employment scale. Net effect: more CC, less routing monopoly.
      Nations eliminating corporations REMOVES a correction node at
        economic scale. Net effect: less CC, more routing monopoly.
    Adding distributed correction capacity is not the same operation
    as concentrating correction capacity, even when both are framed
    as anti-extraction interventions.

    Prevent routing boundary monopoly from forming. This requires
    distributed correction capacity that can fire against the corporate
    node AND the state node independently. Four-condition test applies
    to both: correction pathway exists, structurally independent,
    demonstrated activation under stress, does not require the corrected
    node's permission.

    markets yes, concentration no -- at every scale including the

IMMIGRATION AS EMPLOYER EXTRACTION MECHANISM [structural finding -- v21;
  ethics-costume / many-hosts / CA status / crime-vs-preference /
  oppose-tools-that-shrink extend v25]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A nation is, among other things, a labor market with a routing
  boundary. Control of that boundary determines who competes in
  the labor market, under what conditions, and at what wage floor.
  Control of the boundary is control of who the labor market serves
  -- existing residents or a pool of labor whose entry conditions
  are set by whoever controls the entry mechanism.

  THE COMMON PERSON INTEREST [load-bearing]:
    "For the benefit of the nation" means for the benefit of the
    median resident -- the common person, not the employer class.
    Uncontrolled or selectively enforced immigration serves the
    employer class directly: large labor supply at the bottom of
    the wage distribution depresses wages for everyone competing
    at that level. Cost of depression lands on the common person.
    Benefit lands on the employer extracting from both the
    undocumented worker [maximally, due to ECC] and the legal
    resident competing with them [through wage depression].
    The routing boundary should serve people inside it. The
    Anti-extraction constitutionalist position follows directly:
    routing boundary control = common person interest = national
    interest. Employer interest = porous boundary. These are
    structurally opposed.

  WHY BYPASSING IS WRONG BY DEFINITION:
    Bypassing the formal entry process is not primarily a moral
    failing on the part of the individual immigrant -- many are
    responding rationally to a dysfunctional entry system. But
    it is structurally wrong in the framework sense: it bypasses
    the correction mechanism that makes labor market routing serve
    the common person. The formal entry system is the only
    mechanism that exists for the state to manage who enters the
    labor market and under what conditions. Bypassing it doesn't
    skip a bureaucratic requirement -- it bypasses the routing
    control mechanism entirely, handing the surplus produced by
    that bypass directly to the employer class.
    The individual immigrant is not the primary beneficiary.
    The employer is. The individual immigrant is the instrument;
    the employer is the extraction architecture using the instrument.

  EXIT COST CONSTRUCTION ON UNDOCUMENTED WORKERS:
    Undocumented labor is cheap not because less productive but
    because exit costs are maximally elevated. Cannot leave bad
    employer, report wage theft, organize, or complain without
    triggering deportation risk. Employer captures entire surplus
    that inability to exit produces. Geographic constraint adds
    ECC: cannot move to where wages are higher because movement
    increases ICE exposure risk. Spatial lock produces different
    extraction rates by geography -- ICE enforcement gradient
    as an employer extraction surface by region.
    The employer benefits enormously from this category existing.
    The state benefits from tax revenue both generate. The
    extraction mechanism running on undocumented workers also
    runs on legal residents competing with them -- wage floor
    depression at the bottom of the distribution affects everyone
    in that tier.

  THE LEGITIMATE IMMIGRANT'S STRUCTURAL COMPLAINT [valid]:
    Paid the full entry cost the system demands: time out of labor
    market, legal fees, uncertainty, documentation burden, potential
    family separation during waiting period. Accepted the routing
    boundary's terms. When the boundary is selectively enforced --
    porous for undocumented entry while formally maintained for
    documented entry -- bears the full entry cost while competing
    against people who paid none of it. That asymmetry is an
    extraction mechanism running against the people who followed
    the rules. The formal entry cost becomes a penalty for
    compliance rather than a price for legitimacy.

  THE US SYSTEM AS COUNTER-EXAMPLE [confirmed]:
    Sets entry numbers to benefit employers not median residents.
    Enforces routing boundary selectively -- maintains employer
    leverage through uncertainty. H1B structure at top benefits
    employers sponsoring workers [worker tied to employer, cannot
    exit without losing status -- ECC by visa design]. Formal
    entry pathway dysfunctional enough that bypass is often
    rational for the individual. Result: extraction architecture
    running at every entry tier simultaneously.
    A nation controlling immigration in the common person's
    interest would: set entry numbers based on labor market
    conditions at median and bottom not top; enforce routing
    boundary consistently so formal entry cost isn't a penalty
    for rule-following; remove employer extraction mechanism
    that benefits from undocumented labor specifically; make
    formal pathway functional enough that bypassing it isn't
    the rational choice. US system does the opposite on all four.

  PROBLEM STATEMENT:
    Help that creates or maintains dependence is sometimes
    extraction wearing a generosity legitimacy surface, and
    sometimes genuinely necessary ongoing support. The framework
    does not currently have an instrument distinguishing the two.
    "Help that produces dependence is fake" is the analyst's
    initial framing -- too broad as stated, since some dependence
    is honest and correct [a child depending on a parent; a
    person with a permanent disability depending on ongoing
    support; famine victims depending on emergency aid for the

    extractive specifically when dependence is created or
    maintained where capacity-building was achievable, and the
    helper's own interests [funding continuation, organizational
    survival, donor narrative maintenance, employment] are
    better served by continued dependence than by the recipient
    becoming capable.

  WORKING NAME: MANUFACTURED DEPENDENCE, to distinguish from
    NECESSARY ONGOING SUPPORT. Provisional subtype of EXTRACTION
    [Section Two operational definition] -- the legitimacy
    surface is "help" or "charity"; the captured mechanism is
    the program's design incentive to perpetuate itself rather
    than to resolve the need it claims to address.

  PROVISIONAL FIVE-CONDITION INSTRUMENT [drafted, not yet
  stress-tested against a primary case]:
    [1] CAPACITY-BUILDING WAS ACHIEVABLE: the recipient's
        situation was not structurally permanent [unlike a
        permanent disability or an ongoing famine] -- some
        plausible path to reduced need existed at the time
        the program was designed.
    [2] NO STATED EXIT CONDITION: the program defines success
        by continued enrollment, continued need, or continued
        funding rather than by a stated point at which recipient
        capacity would make the program's presence unnecessary.
        TELL: absence of any exit metric is more diagnostic than
        a low success rate against a stated metric -- a program
        that tries and fails to graduate recipients is different
        in kind from a program that never defines graduation.
    [3] HELPER INTEREST MISALIGNED WITH RESOLUTION: the helper's
        continued operation, funding, or narrative depends on
        the need persisting. Employment, donor pipeline, or
        organizational survival is structurally tied to ongoing
        demand rather than to demand declining.
    [4] LOCAL CAPACITY DISPLACED OR UNDERCUT: existing local
        capability [markets, skills, institutions] is measurably
        weakened or replaced by the program's ongoing presence,
        rather than supplemented toward eventual handoff. Primary
        candidate mechanism: long-running food aid undercutting
        local agricultural markets is the most documented version
        of this in existing aid-sector literature.
    [5] RECIPIENT VOICE ON DEPENDENCE VS EXIT NOT SOUGHT OR NOT
        WEIGHTED: the program does not ask, or does not act on,
        recipient preference between continued support and a
        path to independence. Distinguishes manufactured
        dependence from cases where recipients themselves prefer
        and choose continued support for reasons the helper
        should respect.
    All five required. Absence of [1] [genuinely permanent need]
      converts the case to NECESSARY ONGOING SUPPORT regardless
      of how the other four conditions read.

  HONEST DIFFICULTY [why this is a hard WIP, stated up front]:

Big G [gravitational constant] -- status: UNRESOLVED [tracking]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  G = 6.674 x 10^-11 N⋅m²/kg². Least precisely measured fundamental
  constant in physics. 22 parts per million uncertainty -- orders of
  magnitude worse than Planck's constant or speed of light.
  16+ independent measurements since 1980s. Values scattered and
  mutually inconsistent. April 2026 Schlamminger replication [13 tons
  mercury, decade-long experiment] produced different value from the
  result it attempted to replicate.

  Why measurement is hard:
    Gravity is the weakest force. Signal from lab masses tiny.
    Every surrounding object contributes gravitational noise.
    No way to shield against gravity [unlike EM]. Environmental
    contamination irreducible with current methods.

  Three hypotheses worth tracking:
    H1 [standard]: G is a true constant; scatter reflects
      experimental systematics. Eventually precision improves and
      values converge. Prediction: future replications converge.
      Current status: not happening after 40 years.
    H2 [emergent/secondary]: G is not fundamental but emergent
      from something deeper [Verlinde entropic gravity; G
      derivable from thermodynamic/statistical properties].
      If true: lab-scale scatter expected because you're measuring
      an emergent statistical quantity sensitive to local mass
      configurations. Astronomical scale averages out; lab scale
      doesn't. Prediction: scatter doesn't converge regardless of
      precision improvement.
    H3 [scale-dependent]: gravitational behavior varies at extreme
      scales [MOND territory -- very low acceleration regimes].
      Prediction: lab G and cosmological G measurably different.

  Framework relevance:
    Physics constants as substrate floor [main theory]: if G is
      emergent [H2], the "tower to physics" argument changes.
      G wouldn't be a free parameter or a designed constant --
      it would be a downstream consequence of more fundamental
      structure. Design inference weakens on G specifically; may
      strengthen on whatever G emerges from.
    REFERENCE INTEGRITY: Big G is a measurement surface that
      resists precise calibration. If H2 correct, every
      calculation using G as constant has a soft floor.

  Track: does scatter converge [H1 supported] or persist despite
    precision improvement [H2/H3 supported]?
    Current trajectory: scatter persisting. H1 weakening.

VARIABLE SPEED OF LIGHT IN GRAVITATIONAL FIELD [Li experiment -- tracking]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Enbang Li [University of Wollongong, 2026]: elevator shaft experiment
  using 10km fiber-optic coil. Laser beams fired and reflected back
  through the coil; ultrafast photodetector measured travel time.
  Experiment run at top and bottom of shaft. Temperature control,
  electromagnetic shielding, vibration isolation implemented.
  Result: light moved minutely faster at the bottom of the shaft
  [higher gravitational potential] than at the top.
  Second experiment: 72kg moveable weight positioned near machine.
  Light moved faster when weight was near than when farther away.
  Results published in Scientific Reports.

  HISTORICAL CONTEXT:
    Einstein 1911: proposed light speed changes depending on gravity
    of objects it passes by. When he published general relativity
    in 1915, he abandoned this model. GR works by treating local
    light speed as constant while allowing spacetime curvature --
    producing identical observational predictions at all scales
    previously tested. The abandonment was a design choice to make
    the mathematics tractable, not a falsification. Li is attempting
    to crack open the ambiguity at small local scales where the two
    models might diverge detectably.

  CRITICAL CAVEAT [load-bearing]:
    Li's preliminary results are much larger than Einstein's 1911
    model predicts. The direction is consistent; the magnitude is
    off. Either an uncontrolled noise source remains in the
    experimental setup, or something genuinely new is happening.
    Neither fully resolved. Independent replication required before
    treating as confirmed.

  THREE HYPOTHESES:
    H1 [noise]: magnitude discrepancy reflects uncontrolled
      environmental factor not yet identified. Direction is
      artifact. Prediction: independent replication fails or
      produces null result.
    H2 [genuine but mismodeled]: light speed varies with local
      gravitational potential as Einstein 1911 proposed, but
      Li's experimental calibration needs refinement. Magnitude
      will converge on theoretical prediction with improved
      setup. Prediction: replication produces smaller, consistent
      effect matching Einstein 1911 formula.
    H3 [new physics]: effect is real and larger than Einstein
      1911 predicts because current models are incomplete at
      this scale. Prediction: replication confirms magnitude;
      requires theoretical revision.

  COHERENCE WITH EXISTING GRAVITY CLUSTER:
    If G is emergent [Verlinde entropic gravity] and gravity is
    a statistical thermodynamic phenomenon rather than a
    fundamental force, variable local light speed in a
    gravitational field is structurally expected -- not an
    anomaly but a prediction. The Verlinde framework treats
    gravitational effects as arising from information/entropy
    gradients; local light speed variation would be the optical
    expression of the same underlying structure producing Big G
    scatter. If Li's result survives replication, it provides
    independent experimental support for the H2/H3 cluster in
    the Big G entry above.
    Brans-Dicke connection: if G is coupled to a scalar field,
    and the scalar field varies locally with mass density, local
    light speed variation is a natural consequence of the same
    coupling. Li's experiment may be measuring the optical
    signature of scalar-tensor coupling at lab scale.

  FRAMEWORK RELEVANCE:
    Speed of light as calibration standard: c is the reference
    standard for distance, time, and energy measurement. If c
    varies locally with gravitational potential, every instrument
    using c as a fixed calibration standard has a soft floor in
    gravitational field gradients. GPS, atomic clocks, distance
    measurement, cosmological models all absorb this. The
    measurement surface is built on an assumption that may be
    approximately but not exactly correct.
    GENUINE AXIS producing real outputs through an unexamined
    mechanism: GR produces accurate predictions without requiring
    a decision about whether local light speed is truly constant.
    The formalism absorbs the ambiguity. Li is attempting to force
    the ambiguity into a testable form.
    Practical applications if confirmed: Li notes implications
    for Earth science -- climate monitoring, mineral resource
    exploration. Detecting local gravitational density variations
    via light speed measurement is a new instrument type.

GRAVITY AS CATEGORICALLY SEPARATE FROM QUANTUM MECHANICS [position -- v21]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Standard framing: GR and quantum mechanics are both correct in
  their domains; incompatibility is a problem requiring a unified
  theory [quantum gravity]. The search assumes both are describing
  different scales of the same underlying thing.
  Alternative position: gravity is not incompatible with quantum
  mechanics -- it is categorically separate from it. They are not
  describing different scales of the same thing. They are describing
  genuinely different domains with genuinely different mechanisms
  that do not need unification because they are not in conflict --
  they are not about the same thing.

  THE DOMAINS ARE GENUINELY DISTINCT:
    GR: describes geometry of spacetime at large scales. Continuous,
      relational, large-scale. No claims about discrete particle
      behavior. Its subject is the shape of the space through which
      things move.
    Quantum mechanics: describes behavior of discrete particles and
      fields at small scales. Probabilistic, discrete, individual.
      No claims about spacetime geometry itself. Its subject is the
      behavior of things moving through space.
    The incompatibility only manifests at extreme conditions [black
      hole singularities, Big Bang first moments] where both
      frameworks are simultaneously required and both break down.
      This is not evidence they're the same thing at different
      scales. It may be evidence those conditions are outside both
      domains -- the frameworks reaching their limits, not a sign
      they need unification.

  QUANTUM GRAVITY SEARCH AS CALIBRATION STRIP:
    "Fundamental forces must unify" is the reference standard
    installed by the success of electroweak unification [Weinberg,
    Salam, Glashow, 1979] -- electromagnetic and weak nuclear forces
    unified at high energies. Strong nuclear force partially unified
    [grand unified theories, candidate]. The success of this program
    produced the calibration standard: gravity must also unify with
    quantum field theory.
    The Calibration Strip: the success of unification applied to
    three forces does not confirm that gravity is the same kind of
    thing as those three forces. The application of the unification
    program to gravity assumes the conclusion it needs to demonstrate.
    50+ years of quantum gravity research [string theory, loop
    quantum gravity, others] has produced no confirmed experimental
    prediction. This is not decisive -- fundamental physics can take
    centuries. But the absence of any confirmed prediction after 50+
    years of intensive work is at minimum consistent with the
    hypothesis that the program has the wrong target.

  THE THERMODYNAMIC PARALLEL [Verlinde]:
    Heat and motion appeared incompatible for a long time. The
    resolution was not a unified theory -- it was statistical
    mechanics showing that thermodynamic properties [temperature,
    entropy] are emergent from large numbers of mechanical
    interactions. Temperature is real and follows its own laws;
    it is not reducible to a single mechanical interaction.
    You do not quantize temperature. You explain temperature as
    emergent from quantum interactions at aggregate scale.
    Verlinde's proposal is the same move for gravity: gravity is
    the thermodynamic emergent phenomenon of an underlying
    information-theoretic substrate. It is real and follows its
    own laws [GR]; it is not a fundamental force in the quantum
    sense; it does not need to be quantized because it is not
    quantum at any scale -- it is emergent from quantum-scale
    interactions in the same way temperature is emergent from
    molecular motion.
    If correct: you don't quantize gravity. You explain GR as
    emerging from more fundamental quantum information dynamics.
    The two frameworks are not incompatible -- one is the
    aggregate behavior of the other's substrate.

  ARISTOTELIAN RESIDUE IN GRAVITY LANGUAGE:
    "Natural place" [Aristotle]: things move toward where they
      belong. Teleological -- purpose does explanatory work.
    "Mutual attraction" [Newton]: things move toward each other
      because they're attracted. Replaced purpose with attraction.
      Did not explain what attraction IS at a physical level.
    "Mass curves spacetime" [Einstein]: more precise description.
      Relocates the mystery rather than solving it. What is
      spacetime? What does curvature mean physically? How does
      a massive object communicate its presence to surrounding
      spacetime?
    Each replacement increased mathematical precision while the
    mechanism remained genuinely unknown. The language shifted
    from purpose to attraction to geometry; the underlying
    question [what is actually happening] was never answered.
    Newton said so explicitly: "hypotheses non fingo" -- I frame
    no hypotheses about causes. The math works; the mechanism is
    unknown. This remains true. GR is the most precise description
    of gravity ever produced and its mechanism is genuinely unknown.

  LI EXPERIMENT RELEVANCE:
    If local light speed varies with gravitational potential, that
    is a property of the geometric/thermodynamic domain with no
    quantum implications. A GR-adjacent finding in a categorically
    separate domain -- consistent with gravity being a different
    kind of thing. The finding would be difficult to explain in
    a quantum gravity framework but natural in a Verlinde/
    emergent thermodynamics framework.

  HONEST LIMITS:
    This is a position, not a confirmed finding. Verlinde has
    precision gravitational lensing problems. The categorically
    separate position does not resolve what happens at black hole
    singularities -- it reframes the problem [those conditions
    may be outside both domains] without solving it. The position
    is internally consistent and coherent with available evidence;
    it is not established physics.
    The quantum gravity search may yet produce confirmed
    experimental predictions. Track: any confirmed quantum gravity

    Absence of confirmed prediction after another decade of
    intensive work would strengthen it.
